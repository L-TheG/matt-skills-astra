# Durable scope decisions

Consult relevant existing scope decisions when evaluating a similar enhancement. Prior rejection is evidence, not an automatic veto; distinguish genuinely equivalent requests from superficial similarity.

When the maintainer rejects an enhancement and recording the decision is within scope, reuse the existing decision log or .out-of-scope/<concept>.md. Capture the decision, durable reason, and links to related requests. Do not record an already-implemented feature or temporary deferral as a rejected feature.

When a decision changes, preserve history with a superseding note and link to the new rationale. Do not delete the old record or reopen historical issues automatically.

Posting a rejection, changing labels, and closing an issue require authorization for those tracker actions. Verify each write.
